Create a Future Tech Code of Conduct

Instruction: Complete this extension task to challenge yourself on technology ethics. (Est. Time: 30+ mins)

Task Overview

You’ve explored an exciting new technology and thought about how to use it responsibly. Now imagine your tech becomes super popular – used in schools, homes, shops, and even by the government!

Your challenge is to write a "Future Tech Code of Conduct": a set of 5 rules or promises that people should follow to ensure they use this technology fairly, safely, and ethically.

Guiding Question:

How does this tech collect data? Is it scanning environments, faces, or recording voices?

  • Does it need user consent before capturing information?
  • Should it be turned off in designated private zones (like changing rooms)?

Guiding Question:

Could constant use of this technology isolate people or cause physical strain?

  • How will users balance physical screen time and human relationships?
  • Are there limits or mental disconnect thresholds needed?

Guiding Question:

Can the content produced by this tech be easily manipulated or used to deceive?

  • How can you confirm facts or verify outputs?
  • Should synthetic or AI content be clearly labeled?

Guiding Question:

What kind of permanent digital record is created when people use this?

  • Will the history generated affect users' reputations in the future?
  • Are security logs kept safely encrypted and simple to delete?

Guiding Question:

How can this tool keep relationships positive, both online and offline?

  • How do we prevent virtual bullying, cheating, or intellectual copyright theft?

Code Guidelines Example:

  1. I will not record people without their permission.
  2. I will turn off my glasses in private places (like toilets).
  3. I will double-check any news or images before sharing.
  4. I will take regular screen breaks to protect my eyes and mind.
  5. I will not use smart glasses to cheat or hurt others.
