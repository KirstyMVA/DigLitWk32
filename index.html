Create a Future Tech Code of Conduct

Task Overview:

You’ve explored an exciting new technology and thought about how to use it responsibly. Now imagine your tech becomes super popular – used in schools, homes, shops, and even by the government!

Your challenge is to write a “Future Tech Code of Conduct”: a set of 5 rules or promises that people should follow to use this technology fairly, safely, and ethically.

 

Your Code of Conduct Should:

  • Be based on the technology you researched

  • Include 5 clear “rules” or “guidelines” in simple language

  • Show that you’ve thought about:

    • 📖 Privacy

    • ❤️ Wellbeing

    • 🔍 Truth and fairness

    • 👣 Digital footprint

    • 👥 Treating others with respect

Example (for Smart AR Glasses):

📘 Code of Conduct for Smart Glasses

  1. I will not record people without their permission.

  2. I will turn off my glasses in private places (like toilets or locker rooms).

  3. I will double-check any news or images before sharing.

  4. I will take regular screen breaks to protect my eyes and mind.

  5. I will not use smart glasses to cheat or hurt others.
